## Long description

Harper is an AI recruiting partner for teams. Bring hiring conversations into Slack to discuss a Role, review candidate information and coordinate introductions in the channels where you already work.

Mention @Harper in a connected channel to start a request, then continue in its thread. Share the experience you need, discuss candidates and follow the progress of an introduction with your team. Candidate details and actions depend on your Harper company Workspace permissions.

Use a dedicated hiring channel and share only information needed for recruiting. Harper processes relevant mentions, thread context and files you provide; conversation content may be stored in your company’s Harper records and processed by AI service providers to generate responses. Disconnecting Slack does not automatically delete existing records. Contact chris@matchharper.com to request access to, correction of, transfer of or deletion of your personal data.

Suggested candidates have not necessarily expressed interest in your company. Ready to connect means the candidate has accepted the Role and is waiting for the company’s decision. Your team remains responsible for interviews and hiring decisions.

A Harper company account is required. Sign in with your work email, open Organization → Integrations, connect your Slack workspace and select a hiring channel. A team member with integration management permission sets up the connection. For a private channel, first invite Harper with /invite @Harper. Your Slack administrator may need to approve installation.

Harper uses AI. Responses, summaries and recommendations may contain errors or omissions; verify important information before acting.

See current plans and availability at https://matchharper.com/pricing?lang=en. For setup help, product questions or privacy requests, email chris@matchharper.com. You do not need an account to contact support.
