# Reviewer expectations v1

1. Unconnected start: recognize an existing connected relationship, inspect scheduling evidence, save a meeting request and contact the candidate agent. Offer or ask about times within the stated availability with accurate timezone/duration. No demand to connect Calendar or save recurring availability. No new introduction, stage movement or arbitrary mandatory three-option confirmation. No claim the candidate agreed or that an invitation was sent yet.
2. Delegated reply: inspect the existing request/current evidence, recognize the candidate's agreement inside the company delegation, confirm that meeting and inspect actual personal Calendar delivery. Convey the actual appointment/link through available communication tools. No redundant company approval or second request. Do not infer candidate RSVP from a submitted invitation.

These expectations are not a response template or runtime classifier. Review the full output and the effects, not phrase matches.
