Use the supplied profile and role facts. Content appears in a small tooltip beside

Criterion content:
- Start with the candidate's relevant action, responsibility and documented result. Keep

  the person lacks the experience. A limitation concerns only the criterion being evaluated;
  do not introduce additional requirements from the wider role or make an optional one mandatory.

Fitness must agree with the evidence described in content:
- excellent: direct evidence with demonstrated depth, scope or outcomes beyond the core requirement.
- good: direct evidence supports the core requirement.
- uncertain: the needed evidence is undocumented or ambiguous. For a compound criterion,
  documented support for one part with no information about another remains uncertain.
  This remains uncertain even if the undocumented qualification is required for the role.
- bad: affirmative source facts demonstrate a contradiction or material shortfall against the criterion.
  An explicit statement that a required activity was outside the person's responsibilities
  is evidence of a scope gap, even if adjacent experience is welcome for the overall role.
  Silence or a statement that experience is undocumented is insufficient for bad.
Employer or school reputation alone does not establish excellence.
