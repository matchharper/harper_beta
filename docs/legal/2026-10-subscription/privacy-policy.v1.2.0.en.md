Harper receives data when you register, complete a profile, upload a file, have a conversation, or choose an opportunity. When Harper directly connects a company and talent, Harper may store follow-up communications and progress information exchanged through an email path disclosed to the participants in order to support the connection and understand its stage. Access and security data may be created automatically when you use the service. Live audio is transmitted and processed to provide the call feature. Harper does not separately store the original audio file, but may store the transcript.

### How payment information is processed

Stripe collects full card numbers and card security codes through its payment screen; Harper does not store them on its own servers. Harper stores or retrieves only identifiers, status, and billing details needed to verify payments and operate subscriptions. Billing contacts, customer email, and Workspace identifiers may be sent for payment processing. Candidate resumes and full candidate or company chats are not sent to Stripe for subscription billing.

Authorized team members see information according to their permissions. Billing and credit history require billing management access. When a company connects Slack, connected workspace, channel and user identifiers and necessary conversations and events are processed to provide that integration.

### Statutory retention and account deletion

Data is deleted when its purpose ends. Where law requires transaction records to be kept, only the necessary evidence is retained separately from ordinary service data. Where South Korean electronic-commerce record-retention duties apply to the transaction, the periods are:

| Record | Retention |
| --- | --- |
| Contracts and withdrawal | 5 years |
| Payments and service supply | 5 years |
| Consumer complaints and disputes | 3 years |
| Representations and advertising | 6 months |

Records subject to tax or other statutory duties are retained only to the extent and for the period required by that law. This does not permit blanket retention of all chats, resumes, or candidate information. Data is deleted when the applicable ground and period expire.

Deleting a team member's account is different from closing the company's Workspace. Individual departure does not automatically delete shared company records used by other team members or legally required transaction evidence. Harper will explain the scope of personal-data deletion or anonymization and any records that must remain when handling a request. Subscription cancellation stops renewal and is distinct from a data-deletion request.

## 3. Grounds for processing and the registration checkbox

Harper processes data needed to create and perform the service contract, including account operation, profile management, AI support, and recommendations you request. Security and troubleshooting data is processed only as necessary for Harper's and users' legitimate interests. Company account operation, subscription payments, and allowances are processed as necessary to perform the contract; tax and transaction retention follow applicable law. Where the law requires separate consent, Harper presents the specific details and obtains that consent at the relevant time.

The required registration checkbox records that you reviewed this Privacy Policy. It does not by itself mean that you consented to sharing your profile with a particular company, receiving marketing messages, processing sensitive data, or authorizing recurring paid subscriptions.

## 4. AI features

Harper may send your profile, resume, preferences, conversations, and transcripts to AI providers to generate summaries, responses, profile suggestions, and recommendation reasons. Company features may process hiring requirements, company conversations, and lawfully accessible candidate information as necessary for responses, organization, and review support. During a live call, audio is processed to generate responses and transcripts. Harper does not create voiceprints or use audio for biometric authentication.

AI output is supporting information and does not make a final hiring decision. You may ask Harper to correct or delete inaccurate profile or recommendation information or to have it reviewed by a person.

## 5. Disclosure to recruiting companies and others

Harper does not disclose personal data to a recruiting company without your separate consent or another legal basis. If you choose to connect with a specific company, Harper first identifies that company, the purpose, data fields, retention period, and effect of refusal, and then asks for separate consent on a company-specific screen. If you refuse, only that connection is unavailable; Harper's other core features remain available.

If disclosure is required by law to a court, regulator, or law-enforcement authority, Harper discloses only the data required.

## 7. Cookies and analytics

Harper uses cookies and localStorage for session continuity, language settings, security, and service analytics. Google Analytics and Vercel Analytics may process page and feature events. Harper does not use this data for personalized advertising or sell personal data.

You can delete or block cookies in your browser settings. Blocking essential storage may prevent login or other features from working.

## 8. Your rights, children, and sensitive data

You may request access, correction, deletion, suspension of processing, or withdrawal of consent. Harper verifies the requester and responds under applicable law, and explains any lawful restriction. You may also use the account-deletion feature.

Harper is not directed to children under 14 and does not request sensitive data such as health, disability, religion, political views, or biometric identifiers. Please do not add unnecessary sensitive data to a resume or conversation. Harper will obtain separate consent if a future feature requires sensitive-data processing.

## 9. Safeguards and deletion

Harper applies access restrictions, authentication and authorization controls, encryption in transit, log review, backups, and service-provider management. When a retention period ends or a purpose is completed, the responsible team verifies the data and deletes it without undue delay. Electronic data is deleted so that recovery is impracticable, and backup copies are removed according to the backup cycle. Statutory records are limited to Section 2, retained separately, and not used for unrelated purposes. Harper will explain the outcome of a deletion request and the reason for any records that must remain.

## 10. Changes to this policy

Harper will provide advance notice through the service, email, or another appropriate method before a material change takes effect. Each version is retained with its effective date.
