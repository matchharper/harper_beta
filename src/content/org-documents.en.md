# Documents <!-- id: documents -->

New to Harper? Read **About Harper → Getting started → Company and team → Creating a Role**. If hiring is already underway, start with search settings, connection decisions, or the candidate communication guide.

## About Harper <!-- id: harper-introduction -->

Harper is your recruiting partner. Explain your team's situation and who you need, and Harper helps define the Role, search and review candidates, handle correspondence, and coordinate meetings. Your company decides whom to approach and connect with.

### Two introduction paths <!-- id: harper-introduction-two-introduction-paths -->

| | Suggested candidates | Ready to connect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Candidate intent | Has not accepted this Role. An earlier Harper recommendation or decline may exist | Has heard about the company and Role and agreed to talk |
| Your decision | Request an **Intro** or **Pass** | **Connect** or **Reject** for this Role |
| After acceptance | Candidate acceptance triggers the email introduction to your selected team members without another company approval | Your company accepts the connection and chooses how to connect |
| Access | Shared Free Intros can fund Intro requests | Requires a slot assigned to the Role or access under an existing agreement |

These are more than lists of people matching search filters. Still, appearing in **Suggested candidates** does not establish interest or an application.

### What Ready to connect means <!-- id: harper-introduction-what-ready-to-connect-means -->

Harper introduces the Role to the candidate and checks important missing information when the profile alone is insufficient. Once willingness to talk is confirmed and the introduction has been prepared and delivered, the candidate appears in **Ready to connect**. Acceptance alone does not prove that the introduction has already reached your company.

```callout
Your team's turn to review

The candidate has already agreed to talk with your company and is waiting for your decision. Review the recommendation and materials, then decide whether to connect.
```

### What Intro requested means <!-- id: harper-introduction-what-intro-requested-means -->

A confirmed company Intro request moves the candidate to **Intro requested**. The card distinguishes **preparing the proposal → awaiting the candidate's reply → accepted and being connected**. Silence is neither acceptance nor decline.

After the proposal is actually delivered, Harper can handle additional questions or information sharing. Interviews and ordinary Pipeline moves are unavailable before the candidate accepts.

## Getting started <!-- id: getting-started -->

Prepare **company information, the team members involved in hiring, a Slack channel, and a Role**. A finished job posting is optional.

### Joining a workspace <!-- id: getting-started-joining-a-workspace -->

Self-serve workspace creation for new companies is temporarily paused. If invited, sign in with the invited email and accept the invitation. If your company has a workspace, ask an Owner for an invitation. The same company email domain does not grant membership automatically.

New companies can discuss access with the Harper team through the company page's meeting request or Contact us.

### Initial setup <!-- id: getting-started-initial-setup -->

1. Review company information and Pitch on the [company page](#org-company).
2. [Invite the team members](#org-members) who will review candidates and receive introduction emails. Owners manage membership.
3. Connect Slack in [Integrations](#org-slack) and prepare a notification channel.
4. Open [New role](#org-newRole) to define the work, required experience, and employment conditions.
5. In Role Settings, review **candidate search**, its days and time, the contact person, and the notification channel.

### After registration <!-- id: getting-started-after-registration -->

You do not need to keep the page open. Review Suggested candidates to choose whom to approach, and decide on Ready to connect candidates where the Role has access. Candidates shared with your company are also available in Slack and [Inbox](#org-inbox).

Registering a Role does not mean candidates have already been found or contacted. A search can return few or no suitable candidates.

## Company and team <!-- id: company-and-team -->

Manage company-wide information on the [company page](#org-company), and Role-specific hiring criteria in the Role workspace.

### Pitch and Context for Harper <!-- id: company-and-team-pitch-and-context-for-harper -->

**Pitch** explains what your company builds and the concrete achievements, culture, and benefits that make it worth joining. Actual products, working practices, and benefit conditions are more useful than general praise.

**Context for Harper** holds lasting company or Role background. Put Role-specific requirements in Hiring Brief and individual candidate situations in the candidate's records. One person's situation is not a company-wide hiring rule.

Ask Harper to read saved company information or context. Review proposed changes before updates to Hiring Brief or memory are applied.

### Team member permissions <!-- id: company-and-team-team-member-permissions -->

| Permission | Responsibilities |
| --- | --- |
| Owner | Invitations, removals, permissions, workspace management, and billing where available |
| Admin | Candidates, Roles, company information, integrations, and connection decisions |
| Viewer | Reviewing company and candidate information |

Slack uses the linked team member's permissions. Joining a channel does not grant edit access.

### Response language <!-- id: company-and-team-response-language -->

The Korean or English language chosen on the web is saved per team member and used by default in their Slack conversations. You can request another language for a reply. Workspace-wide announcements and recurring introductions use Korean for a Korean headquarters and English otherwise.

Changing the response language does not automatically translate Role documents or candidate correspondence.

## Creating a Role <!-- id: create-a-role -->

Share a job link or file, or describe the hiring background and person you need. Use **New role** on the web or ask Harper to start a new Role in Slack.

### What to share <!-- id: create-a-role-what-to-share -->

- **Company and team:** Why you are hiring and the team's situation
- **Work:** Problems to solve, responsibility, and expected outcomes
- **Experience:** Requirements, preferences, and disqualifying conditions
- **Employment conditions:** Location, work mode, employment type, compensation range, visa or language needs, and timing

```quote
Example

“We need our first data engineer. Experience leading data pipeline development and Python/SQL are required. The Role is hybrid in Seoul, and we're open to discussing compensation for a strong candidate.”
```

Explain undecided conditions as open questions. Distinguishing **required** from **preferred** helps Harper represent your criteria accurately.

### Description and Hiring Brief <!-- id: create-a-role-description-and-hiring-brief -->

**Description** explains the work, team, and employment conditions to candidates. **Hiring Brief** holds the internal criteria for deciding whom to interview. These are the initial documents for a new Role; **Evaluation Criteria** can be added when a separate review framework is needed.

Updating one document does not automatically rewrite every other document. Review the affected scope when public facts or internal criteria change.

### Example profiles and reference people <!-- id: create-a-role-example-profiles-and-reference-people -->

If the desired level is hard to explain, share a reference person's **LinkedIn, GitHub, or professional materials** and the strengths to use as a benchmark. Harper uses that evidence to clarify the Role rather than automatically treating the person as a candidate.

Give reasons for positive or negative feedback on prepared example profiles. These examples help calibrate criteria; they do not mean real candidates have been contacted. A rating without a reason does not create a new requirement.

### Before registering and after editing <!-- id: create-a-role-before-registering-and-after-editing -->

Check the public description, required and preferred criteria, primary contact, and Slack channel. Later, tell Harper what changed and why so you can review the proposed update.

```example
Role conversation examples

Fintech experience is now preferred, not required. Show the Hiring Brief update.
I like the scope of this example profile's product launch work. Prioritize that level of responsibility.
```

Candidates already introduced may have been reviewed under earlier criteria. Review them again if the requirements changed significantly.

## Managing candidate search <!-- id: candidate-search -->

Recurring search settings, requesting a fresh search, and reading existing candidates are different actions.

### Recurring search settings <!-- id: candidate-search-recurring-search-settings -->

Role Settings lets you enable candidate search and set its days and time. New Roles default to enabled at 9 AM on Monday, Wednesday, and Friday in Asia/Seoul. Existing Roles keep their saved settings.

This controls recurring searches for company-reviewed suggestions. Turning it off does not block direct search requests or disable all candidate-first recommendations available under a slot or existing agreement. Setting changes do not cancel searches already started or proposals already delivered.

### Run Search <!-- id: candidate-search-run-search -->

Use **Run Search** in the Role conversation or ask Harper to find new candidates from the saved current Hiring Brief. It searches for people your company might approach without waiting for the recurring schedule.

A search alone does not send a company proposal. Outreach begins after your team reviews a candidate and confirms an **Intro request**. If nobody is suitable, the result can be empty.

### Search history and limits <!-- id: candidate-search-search-history-and-limits -->

Ask Harper when recent searches ran and how many people were reviewed. Older records can also be requested. Selection totals are not delivery, readership, or acceptance counts; missing counts are not zero.

When the Role reaches its review limit, some new candidate-first outreach is limited. Deciding on existing Ready to connect candidates can free capacity. This does not mean all Suggested candidates searches stop.

## Reviewing candidates <!-- id: review-recommendations -->

Suggested candidates asks whether to approach someone; Ready to connect asks whether to connect. Read the current state and confirmed candidate intent together.

### What to review <!-- id: review-recommendations-what-to-review -->

- **TL;DR:** Verified experience, individual contribution, and responsibility
- **Harper Note:** Shareable context about desired work, responsibility, working conditions, or joining timing
- **Overall and criterion-level fit:** Evidence relevant to the saved Role criteria and remaining questions
- **Materials and records:** Resumes, correspondence, and activity currently shared with your company

Assessments aid your review; they do not guarantee an application, acceptance, or successful hire. Focus on actual problems and responsibility rather than company names, and turn missing information into questions to confirm.

### Sharing scope and additional questions <!-- id: review-recommendations-sharing-scope-and-additional-questions -->

Suggested candidates and unaccepted Intro requested proposals show limited profiles. Even when additional contact is available, private resumes and contact details are not automatically disclosed. Private compensation figures and conversations with other companies are not shared.

Ask [Harper to check important missing information](#ask-harper). Ready to connect candidates are waiting for your decision. Assign reviewers and a final decision-maker within your team.

## Introduction and connection decisions <!-- id: accept-or-decline -->

**Intro requests**, **Pass**, **Connect**, and **Reject** have different effects on the candidate.

### Intro request and Pass <!-- id: accept-or-decline-intro-request-and-pass -->

An Intro request needs **reasons you want to meet** and **company recipients for the introduction after acceptance**. Once you review and confirm the request, Harper introduces the company and Role. Candidate acceptance triggers the email introduction without another company connection approval. A separate process stage is optional.

Pass removes the suggestion without contacting or notifying the candidate.

### Connect in Ready to connect <!-- id: accept-or-decline-connect-in-ready-to-connect -->

The default **Email intro** addresses the candidate and CCs your selected team members. If a recipient is missing, an Owner must [invite them](#org-members) first. Recipients should reply all to greet the candidate and explain the next step.

**Direct contact** sends no introduction email; your team must make the first contact and arrange the next conversation. Accepting a connection does not confirm an interview time.

### Reject in Ready to connect <!-- id: accept-or-decline-reject-in-ready-to-connect -->

Reject ends the process for this Role rather than putting it on hold. No introduction email is sent; the ending becomes visible to the candidate and Harper communicates it. Information already shown or delivered cannot be recalled.

If information is missing, ask for [questions or materials](#ask-harper) before declining.

```callout
Decision reasons

Connection note and Reject reason are optional and are not sent verbatim to the candidate. Leave the actual reason so it can inform future recommendations.
```

## Candidate communication <!-- id: ask-harper -->

Ask Harper in `/org` or Slack to handle questions, material requests, or information sharing before a Ready to connect decision or during an active process after connection. Additional contact is also available in Intro requested after proposal delivery while a reply is pending.

### What to request <!-- id: ask-harper-what-to-request -->

Specify **the candidate, Role, decision, and information you need**. Ask about real responsibility, working availability, or an updated resume. For information such as compensation, obtain a fresh response with the candidate's chosen sharing scope.

```example
Contact examples

Ask whether this candidate personally led payment incident response and what they were responsible for.
Add the requirement for two office days a week in Seoul to the proposal already sent.
Ask for a resume covering their recent work.
```

### Direct messages and reviewed drafts <!-- id: ask-harper-direct-messages-and-reviewed-drafts -->

Clear routine correspondence can be conveyed directly. First outreach or a proposal where wording and company positioning matter may need a reviewed draft. Creating or editing a draft does not send it.

Once you approve the displayed final copy, it is normally delivered after a short delay. An **explicit send-now request** allows immediate delivery. A pending contact can be canceled only while cancellation is available; delivered messages cannot be recalled.

### Replies and follow-ups <!-- id: ask-harper-replies-and-follow-ups -->

Replies are reported in the `/org` conversation or Slack thread where the request began. Candidates control their response and sharing scope and may decline or not respond.

For a still-valid request for an answer or material, Harper can send one follow-up email at least three days after initial delivery. Information-only messages do not all require a follow-up. Review recent correspondence when requesting another contact.

Additional contact does not establish candidate acceptance, a connection, or a company hiring decision.

## Pipeline and scheduling <!-- id: pipeline -->

Manage connected candidates in **Pipeline** from the first conversation to the final decision.

### Process records and correspondence <!-- id: pipeline-process-records-and-correspondence -->

Create stages for your process and move candidates to match actual progress. Record verified facts, remaining questions, and the next responsible team member. Moving a stage alone sends no scheduling request, email, or Harper message.

Ask Harper to communicate the next step or send it directly. An earlier confirmed meeting and delivery of a new invitation are separate facts.

### Coordinating interviews <!-- id: pipeline-coordinating-interviews -->

The organizer connects Google Calendar and saves availability in [Settings → Calendar](#org-calendar). Harper excludes busy times, sends options to the candidate, and delivers Calendar invitations and a Google Meet link after a time is selected.

Specify the candidate, Role, and conversation purpose. Candidates who have not accepted a company proposal must accept first. Updating availability does not automatically change existing meetings.

### Pausing and ending a Role <!-- id: pipeline-pausing-and-ending-a-role -->

**Pause** stops new recommendations while existing candidates remain reviewable. **End** stops new recommendations and ends the Role. Notices are not all sent at the moment of the status change; after a grace period, accepted candidates still in progress receive an ending notice and their process closes. Final-offer stages are excluded from this automatic closure.

Ending one candidate's process is different from ending the whole Role. Sent emails remain, so the company contact should also communicate directly if conversations or meetings are underway.

## Slots and Intros <!-- id: slots-and-credits -->

An Intro is a unit used to request an introduction or accept a connection. An Intro request is the action; the Intro balance measures available usage, rather than completed introductions. Use 1 Intro and 2 Intros for quantities.

Role count, paid-feature access, and Intro balance are separate limits. Existing agreements retain their agreed conditions.

### Standard access <!-- id: slots-and-credits-standard-access -->

| Plan | Role count | Intros and features |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Free | 1 active Role; unlimited drafts | 10 monthly Intros shared across all Roles |
| Slot | 1 active Role per slot, plus 1 Free Role | Paid features and 50 monthly Intros for the assigned Role; the shared 10 remain separate |
| Enterprise or existing agreement | Agreed terms | Check your company's contract |

Ready to connect requires an assigned slot or access under an existing agreement. Shared Intros alone do not unlock it. Free and standard slot subscriptions have no hiring success fee.

### Spending and renewal <!-- id: slots-and-credits-spending-and-renewal -->

On standard plans, **confirming an Intro request** and **Connect in Ready to connect** each spend one Intro. The Role's own slot Intros are used first, then shared Intros. Another Role's slot Intros cannot fund it.

Intros renew monthly and do not roll over. Annual billing still uses monthly Intro renewal. Exhausted Intros do not prevent creating or activating Roles.

### Assignment and access expiry <!-- id: slots-and-credits-assignment-and-access-expiry -->

When a slot expires, its paid features end. After assigning remaining slots, the oldest eligible Free Role stays active and surplus Free Roles are paused. Existing candidates, Intros and progress history are preserved. Review the previous Role's access when reassigning a slot.

```callout
Current billing access

Self-serve signup for new companies and billing for ordinary workspaces are temporarily paused. Where Slots and Billing are available, they show assignments, subscriptions, and invoices. If those menus are absent or your agreement is unclear, contact the Harper team through Contact us. Public offers do not establish your company's actual contract or balance.
```

## External jobs and company-name disclosure <!-- id: public-job-visibility -->

Manage **external job visibility** and **company-name disclosure** in Role Settings. Review the public Description as well.

### External job visibility <!-- id: public-job-visibility-external-job-visibility -->

Enabling the setting lets Harper promote the Role on channels such as LinkedIn. It does not prove a posting is already live. Turning it off does not delete the internal Role or stop hiring.

### Hiding the company name <!-- id: public-job-visibility-hiding-the-company-name -->

You can hide the company name. Review public Description text for identifying details as well.

```callout
Existing external postings

Changing disclosure or visibility settings does not automatically update every existing external posting. Tell the Harper team which posting needs to change.
```
