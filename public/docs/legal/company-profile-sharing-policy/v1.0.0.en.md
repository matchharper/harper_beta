# Consent to Share Personal Information with {{company_name}}

Harper will share the following personal information with {{company_name}} only with your consent.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Recipient | {{company_name}} |
| Purpose | Recruitment review, interviews, and the hiring process |
